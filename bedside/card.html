Darrow–Yannet, read backward

Sodium is two problems, not one. The amount of sodium sets ECF volume and threatens the circulation; its concentration sets tonicity and threatens the brain.

ICF (cells) ECF normal
Ask in order
1
Brain or circulation in danger?
Circulation → volume. Brain → tonicity.
2
ECF volume up or down?
Bedside: orthostatics, JVP, edema, mucous membranes, weight.
3
Is Na⁺ low, normal or high?
A concentration: tonicity, not volume. Check glucose.
4
Water into or out of cells?
Low tonicity → swell; high → shrink. Brain cells matter most.
5
What is the kidney doing?
Read it from the urine.
The kidney's vote
